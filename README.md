# HAPPY NEW YEAR 2027 — NANDITA JAYSWAL
### Premium Black & Metallic Gold Cinematic Greeting Website

An extraordinary, cinematic digital New Year 2027 greeting experience created especially for **NANDITA JAYSWAL**. Built with pure vanilla HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and Web Audio API—no build tools, Node.js, or external frameworks required.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Two-Page Pure Static Architecture**
   - `index.html`: Atmospheric cinematic welcome experience with floating stardust, ambient golden halo, and twin celestial stars.
   - `celebration.html`: Full-screen realistic night sky fireworks simulation with interactive particle physics, tap-to-burst anywhere, and "✦ MAKE A WISH ✦" interaction.
2. **Subtle Friendship & Affection Storytelling**
   - Harmonious twin golden stars tethered by shimmering light threads.
   - Interactive twin lights on the celebration page representing warmth, mutual appreciation, and connection without uncomfortable confessions.
3. **Original Instrumental Melody (Web Audio API)**
   - Custom synthesized peaceful and celebratory score (Dmaj9, Bm9, Gmaj7, Aadd9 chords with harp arpeggios, celesta bells, and warm pads).
   - Audio ON/OFF button with real-time animated equalizer bars.
   - Built to comply with Android and iOS mobile autoplay policies.
4. **Interactive Fireworks Simulation**
   - 7 distinct firework variations (Chrysanthemum, Champagne bursts, Radial willow, Comet trails, Glittering cascades, Grand finale).
   - Tap or click anywhere on the sky to launch fireworks directly to that point.
   - Dedicated "✦ MAKE A WISH ✦" button that detonates a center sky grand finale and delivers an elegant golden affirmation card.
5. **Exact Dedicated Message Preserved**
   - Dedicated exclusively to **NANDITA JAYSWAL**.
   - Year: **2027**.

## 📱 Android Mobile Preview Instructions (Acode / Chrome)

Follow these simple steps to preview this website on your Android phone:

1. **Download & Extract**:
   - Download the project files (`Nandita-New-Year-2027.zip`).
   - Open your Android **Files** / **File Manager** app.
   - Locate the ZIP file in your **Downloads** folder and tap **Extract** (or Extract to `Nandita-New-Year-2027`).
2. **Open in Acode**:
   - Open the **Acode** app on your phone.
   - Tap the side menu (≡) and select **Open Folder**.
   - Navigate to your extracted `Nandita-New-Year-2027` folder and tap **Use this folder** (Allow permission).
3. **Launch Preview**:
   - In Acode, tap on `index.html` to open it.
   - Tap the **Play / Preview (▶)** button at the top right of Acode, OR choose "Open in Browser" (Google Chrome).
4. **Experience the Celebration**:
   - Enjoy the floating stardust and golden typography on Page 1.
   - Tap **"ENABLE MUSIC ♫"** at the top right to start the peaceful ambient melody.
   - Tap **"LET'S BEGIN THE CELEBRATION →"** to transition to `celebration.html`.
   - On Page 2, tap anywhere on the night sky to launch custom fireworks!
   - Tap **"✦ MAKE A WISH ✦"** to trigger the grand finale.
   - Read the heartfelt message and tap **"EXPERIENCE AGAIN"** to replay anytime.

## 🚀 Free GitHub Pages Deployment Guide

Deploy this project for free on GitHub Pages in under 3 minutes:

1. **Create GitHub Repository**:
   - Go to [github.com](https://github.com) and sign in.
   - Click the green **New** button to create a new repository.
   - Name your repository (for example: `happy-new-year-2027` or `nandita-new-year`).
   - Set visibility to **Public**.
   - Leave "Initialize with README" unchecked and click **Create repository**.
2. **Upload Files**:
   - On the repository page, click **uploading an existing file**.
   - Drag and drop or upload all files from the `Nandita-New-Year-2027` folder:
     - `index.html` (Must be directly in the repository root)
     - `celebration.html`
     - `styles.css`
     - `welcome.js`
     - `celebration.js`
     - `assets/` folder
   - Click **Commit changes**.
3. **Enable GitHub Pages**:
   - In your repository, click the **Settings** tab.
   - In the left sidebar, click **Pages**.
   - Under **Build and deployment** > **Branch**:
     - Select branch: `main`
     - Select folder: `/(root)`
     - Click **Save**.
4. **Access Your Live Website**:
   - Wait 30–60 seconds.
   - Refresh the Pages tab to see your live URL:
     `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repository-name>/`
   - Share the link with Nandita for an unforgettable New Year celebration!
